Methodology & Limitations

  • Dataset constraints: Trained on a synthetic/historical dataset representing 1,000 U.S. high school students.
  • Generalization risk: The model may not generalize to students outside the specific demographic and geographic distribution of the training set.
  • No causality: Feature correlation does not imply causality (e.g., test preparation courses are highly correlated with success, but other unmeasured factors like motivation may confound this).
